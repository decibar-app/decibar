How call ducking works.

A call app starts using the microphone when a call begins. An app that watches for that can lower every other app until the microphone stops, then bring them back.

Decibar watches which apps use the microphone without recording anything, so it doesn’t need microphone permission. You choose which apps count as calls.

Common questions.

Does macOS lower other audio during calls?

No. macOS has no setting for it, so music and videos keep playing at the same level.

Which call apps work?

Any app that uses the microphone. You choose which apps count as calls, so Zoom, Teams, FaceTime, Slack, Discord and browser calls can all work.

Does Decibar listen to my calls?

No. It notices that an app is using the microphone, without recording or reading the audio, and doesn’t ask for microphone permission.

Does the music come back after the call?

Yes. When the call app stops using the microphone, other apps return to their levels.
