Which one fits your desk?

MonitorControl suits brightness and volume

Free

To start.

  • You want brightness, contrast and volume from one free app
  • You’d like an open-source app
  • Your Mac can’t run macOS 15, or has an Intel chip

Its README says DDC doesn’t work through the built-in HDMI port of M1 Macs and the entry-level M2 Mac mini. It suggests USB-C there.

Decibar suits sound on a desk

$9.99

For Pro. The free version keeps working.

  • You want to go below the monitor’s minimum
  • You want a volume for each app, or EQ for thin speakers
  • You want music to drop during calls

Decibar doesn’t handle brightness. Its software volume doesn’t rely on DDC/CI.
