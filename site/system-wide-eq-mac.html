What macOS gives you.

  1. Music’s equalizer

    Window > Equalizer in Music shapes Music playback and nothing else.

  2. Nothing in Sound settings

    System Settings > Sound has no bass, treble or EQ controls.

  3. Audio MIDI Setup

    It sets formats and levels for devices, with no EQ.

Why the curve should follow the speaker.

Laptop speakers, headphones and monitor speakers each sound different. A curve that adds bass to thin monitor speakers will sound boomy on headphones.

An equalizer that keeps one curve for each output switches with you, so plugging in headphones doesn’t carry over the monitor’s bass boost.

Common questions.

Does macOS have a system-wide equalizer?

No. The equalizer in Music only affects Music. Other apps play without one.

Can I save a different EQ for headphones and speakers?

In Decibar, yes. Each output keeps its own curve, and it switches when the output changes.

Can I make monitor speakers sound fuller?

An EQ can add bass and lower harshness. Decibar has a Monitor Speakers preset as a starting point.

Can I use headphone correction profiles?

Decibar imports a parametric EQ file for your headphones, such as one from AutoEq.
