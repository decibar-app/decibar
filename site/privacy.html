Privacy Policy

Decibar has no accounts, analytics or tracking. Your settings stay on your Mac.

Audio

Decibar doesn’t record, save or send audio. While it’s processing sound, audio passes through a shared buffer on your Mac, which is cleared when playback stops. The buffer can be opened only by your user account and the system’s audio service. Other apps running under your account could read it while audio plays.

What leaves your Mac

  • Update checks. Decibar asks decibar.app for the latest version once a day. The request names the app and its version. It holds nothing that identifies you.
  • Monitor compatibility list. Decibar downloads a list of monitor fixes from decibar.app once a day. It sends nothing about your monitors.
  • Diagnostics. Only if you choose Copy Diagnostics or Email Support. The report lists your Mac model, macOS version, license type, audio settings, the output in use, the apps using audio, and each monitor’s model and serial number. You see it before anything is sent.

The website’s server may keep standard request logs, such as IP addresses, for those downloads.

Purchases

Payment is handled by our payment provider, which receives your payment details under its own privacy policy. We receive your email address to send your license key. The key holds that email address and is checked on your Mac, without contacting a server.
