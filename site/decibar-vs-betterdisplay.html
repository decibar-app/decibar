Which one fits your desk?

BetterDisplay suits display control

$21.99

For Pro.

  • You need HiDPI scaling, XDR brightness or virtual screens
  • You control a TV or AV receiver
  • You’re on an Intel Mac

Pro is a perpetual license with at least one year of updates, and a 14-day trial. Prices vary by region.

Decibar suits sound alone

$9.99

For Pro. The free version keeps working.

  • You only want your monitor’s sound sorted
  • You want a volume for each app
  • You want music to drop during calls

Software volume is free here, and Pro covers all 1.x updates.
