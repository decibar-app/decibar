What if the slider doesn’t move the volume?

Turn on DDC/CI in the monitor’s on-screen menu, then choose Search Again in Decibar. Docks and adapters sometimes block DDC/CI, so try a direct cable. Help has the full steps.

What if the monitor can’t be controlled at all?

Settings > Monitors offers software volume instead, which the free version includes. It adds an audio driver the first time you turn it on.

Can I go below the monitor’s lowest setting?

Yes. Software volume takes audio down to −40 dB, and the volume keys move it in sixteen steps.

Can I send an app to a different device?

Yes, with Pro. The speaker button beside an app sends it to any connected device, or to several at once, and those devices wait for the slowest so you don’t hear an echo. Apps with their own speaker setting need it set to the system output first.

Why isn’t Decibar in the Mac App Store?

Monitor control uses a private macOS API, which the App Store doesn’t allow. Decibar is sold directly and updates itself.

Does Decibar record my audio?

No. It doesn’t save audio or send it anywhere, and it needs no microphone or recording permission.

Why does it ask for my administrator password?

macOS asks when Decibar installs its audio driver. Software volume, per-app volume and the Pro audio features need the driver. Controlling the monitor’s own volume doesn’t. Help explains why.

Why does it want Accessibility permission?

The volume keys need it.

How do I remove Decibar?

Move it to the Trash. If it was in an Applications folder, the driver removes itself within a minute. Help covers the other cases.
