What’s new

  • Volume for each device

  • An output for each app

    Pro

    Send an app to any connected device, and it stays there until you change it.

  • Several outputs at once

    Pro

    Apps now sit under the device they play on, and move when you send them elsewhere.

  • Software volume for each output

    Each HDMI or DisplayPort output keeps its own level.
