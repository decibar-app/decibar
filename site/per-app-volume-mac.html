How to change the volume for each app on a Mac

macOS has one volume for everything, so a loud video and a quiet call share the same level.

What macOS gives you.

  1. One system slider

    Control Center and the volume keys change every app at once.

  2. Sliders inside some apps

    Music, browsers and call apps have their own, but each one hides it somewhere different.

  3. Nothing in Sound settings

    System Settings > Sound has no list of apps and no way to set one quieter.

Why apps share one level.

Every app plays into the same output, and macOS sets one volume on that output. It doesn’t keep a level for each app, and Audio MIDI Setup doesn’t add one.

To give each app its own level, something has to sit between the apps and the output and scale each stream on its own. That takes an audio driver or a system audio extension.

Common questions.

Can macOS set a different volume for each app?

No. macOS has one volume for each output, shared by every app. Some apps have their own slider, but macOS has no list of apps to set.

Does per-app volume work on any output?

In Decibar, yes. It applies to the speakers, headphones, Bluetooth and HDMI or DisplayPort monitors, and each connected device also has its own slider.

Can an app go louder than 100%?

In Decibar, up to 150%, for apps that play too quietly even at full volume.

Do I need to grant microphone access?

Not for Decibar. Its per-app volume works without microphone or screen recording permission.

Is per-app volume free?

Yes. Per-app volume is part of Decibar’s free version. Pro adds EQ, an output for each app, and lowering other audio during calls.
